## 🚀 Things I've built

### 🟣 Argon: a job-search assistant that reads the whole market for you
Pulls job ads from Telegram channels, WhatsApp groups and job sites, scores every ad from 0 to 100 against your CVs, helps you apply, and tells you which skills the market wants next.
`Python` `Ollama` `SQLite / PostgreSQL` `Telegram` `WhatsApp` `Gmail`

<a href="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/argon.html"><img src="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/img/argon/03-market-for-you.webp" width="100%" alt="Argon market page" /></a>

**[Read the Argon case study →](https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/argon.html)** · code private while the beta gets ready

### 🟠 Studio: clean, matching product photos, on your own computer
Drop in ordinary phone photos. Studio removes the background with a small AI model on the CPU, fixes light and colors, and gives every photo the same size, margin and shadow. Fully offline, open source (MIT). Tested on real photos with known problems: dark and blurry photos caught 8 of 8, and one tap on a white spot cuts color error from 10.6 to 3.5.
`Python` `ONNX Runtime` `IS-Net` `U²-Net` `NumPy` `Pillow` `FastAPI`

<a href="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/studio.html"><img src="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/img/studio/01-batch-8-photos.webp" width="100%" alt="Studio with a batch of cleaned product photos" /></a>

**[Source code](https://github.com/mahmoudyosrimahmoud13/studio)** · **[Case study](https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/studio.html)**

### 🔴 Rubies: one bag, many boutiques, one delivery
A marketplace for Alexandria's independent fashion shops. One courier collects from every shop, a QR code confirms the handover, and customers pay cash only for what they keep. Four apps in one (customer, shop, courier, admin), Arabic first.
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Next.js` `Tailwind` `Playwright`

<a href="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/rubies.html"><img src="https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/img/rubies/01-home.webp" width="100%" alt="Rubies home page" /></a>

**[Read the Rubies case study →](https://mahmoudyosrimahmoud13.github.io/rubies.html)** · code private

### 🔵 Hydro Alex: a plumbing shop, open online
An Arabic-first online store for one sanitary ware and plumbing shop in Alexandria. Search by product or by pipe size (half inch, 1/2" or 20 mm), order with cash on delivery, and the owner runs orders, stock, discounts and money reports from one panel.
`FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Next.js` `Tailwind` `Playwright` `Docker`
