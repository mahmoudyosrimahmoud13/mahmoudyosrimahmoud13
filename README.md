### 🟢 inPace: learn at your pace, prove you kept it
A free app for **Windows, Mac, Linux and Android** that walks you through any course step by step and lets someone you trust check that you're really keeping up. Everything stays on your device: no account, no tracking.

* **Any material becomes a course:** YouTube or Udemy links (cut into lessons at their chapters), a messy class folder sorted for you, a PDF book split into chapters, or a course made with AI.
* **Proof that holds up:** milestones are signed with Ed25519 and chained, your checker approves them through links on WhatsApp or Telegram, and verified exams are graded by the checker's app.
* **Keeps you going:** reminders and alarms, daily review, weekly goals, races with friends, roadmaps, and Tempo's island, a turtle whose island grows as you learn.
* **The Android app is the whole app,** not a lite version: a Flutter shell that runs the same Python server on the phone.
* In **14 languages** including Arabic, open source (MIT), with **330+ tests** on three systems and signed builds for four on every release.

### 🟠 Studio: clean, matching product photos, on your own computer
Drop in ordinary phone photos. Studio removes the background with a small AI model on the CPU, fixes light and colors, and gives every photo the same size, margin and shadow. Fully offline, open source (MIT). Tested on real photos with known problems: dark and blurry photos caught 8 of 8, and one tap on a white spot cuts color error from 10.6 to 3.5.
`Python` `ONNX Runtime` `IS-Net` `U²-Net` `NumPy` `Pillow` `FastAPI`

### 🟪 CodeNote: a notebook for developers
Text, code, Markdown and images in one note, on **Android, Windows, Linux and macOS**. Open source (AGPL), local-only: no account, no cloud.

* **58 languages** with real highlighting and their logos, and HTML, CSS and JS that run right from the note.
* **AI that respects your privacy:** explain code, find bugs, and ask questions answered from your own notes. Works with Claude, any OpenAI-compatible API, or fully offline with Ollama or LM Studio.
* **Made for the keyboard:** command palette (Ctrl+K), templates, GitHub Gist import and export, code shared as images, scan code from a photo.
* **Safe notes:** backup and restore in one file, daily backup to any folder, trash with undo, reminders as real notifications.

`Flutter` `BLoC` `Clean Architecture` `Hive` `GetIt` `ML Kit` `GitHub Actions`
